# My Life Calendar 🌱

A life calendar where each square represents one week. Color in the weeks you've lived, write notes, look back on memories, and plan ahead.

## What you can do

### 🎨 Color weeks

* Choose a color (Joy, Calm, Love, etc.) and click or drag your mouse over the squares. You can create your own categories and colors in the **My Colors** section.

### 📝 Write notes for each week

You can make text bold, change its color, highlight it, and add emojis.

### 🗓️ Select multiple weeks at once

* Click on the first week, then hold `Shift` and click on the last week to select the entire range.
* To select individual weeks, hold `Cmd` (Mac) or `Ctrl` (Windows) and click on the weeks you want.

### 📸 Add a title, notes, and one photo to each year

### 🎯 Plan goals for 5 years

* Start with a vision of who you want to become, then outline the steps for each year.
* After 5 years, the calendar will suggest reviewing your progress and reflecting on the journey.
* Previous goals will be saved in **Past Journeys**.

### 🔎 Search notes

* Search finds words in weekly notes, year titles, and year notes.

### ⏳ Remember what happened last year

* The **On This Week** section shows what you wrote about the same week one year ago.

### ↩️ Undo and redo changes

* Use the **Undo** and **Redo** buttons on the page or keyboard shortcuts:
  * `Cmd/Ctrl + Z` to undo
  * `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + Z` to redo.

### 📂 Collapse years for convenience

* Hover over a year, and an arrow will appear on the left that lets you collapse it to free up space on the screen.

## How to get started

1. Download the `my-life-calendar.html` file.
2. Double-click it to open it in your default browser (or open your browser and drag the file into the window).
3. Enter your date of birth, and the calendar will be generated automatically.

You don't need to install anything, and you don't need an internet connection.

### ⚠️ Important: backups

Your data is stored only in your browser. It will be lost if you:

* Clear your browsing history and site data.
* Open the file in a different browser or on another device.
* Use incognito mode.

### 💾 What to do

* After each session, click **Export Calendar**. A ZIP file containing your notes, colors, goals, and photos will be saved.
* To restore or transfer your calendar, click **Import Calendar** and select this ZIP file.

Warning: importing replaces the current calendar data in your browser. Don't share your ZIP file with anyone, as it contains all your entries.
